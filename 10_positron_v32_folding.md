# 10 - Positron V3.2 - Folding

This guide walks you through the procedures of folding the Positron V3.2.

> Original: [LDO Motion Guide](https://ldomotion.com/guides/10---positron-v32---folding)

## 1. Instructions

### Unload the Filament

- Before folding your printer, please remember to unload the filament first. Click Extrude and then Unload.
  - ![Preview](img/10/unload2.preview.png)
  [Large](img/10/unload2.png)
  - ![Preview](img/10/unload1.preview.png)
  [Large](img/10/unload1.png)

### Remove the Glass Bed

- Remove the glass bed by pulling it outwards.
  - ![Preview](img/10/0002-1.preview.png)
  [Large](img/10/0002-1.png)
  - ![Preview](img/10/0001-9.preview.png)
  [Large](img/10/0001-9.png)

### Remove the Bed V Holder

- Unscrew the thumb screw and remove the bed V holder.
  - ![Preview](img/10/0004-3.preview.png)
  [Large](img/10/0004-3.png)
  - ![Preview](img/10/0003-2.preview.png)
  [Large](img/10/0003-2.png)

### Position the Z Idler Latch

- Move the idler latch downward and align it with the linear rail.
  - ![Preview](img/10/0006-10.preview.png)
  [Large](img/10/0006-10.png)
  - ![Preview](img/10/0005-3.preview.png)
  [Large](img/10/0005-3.png)

### Remove the Thumb Screw

- Remove the thumb screw at the bottom of the Z column.
  - ![Preview](img/10/0008.preview.png)
  [Large](img/10/0008.png)
  - ![Preview](img/10/0007-5.preview.png)
  [Large](img/10/0007-5.png)

### Move the Toolhead and Bed Carriage Block

- Move the toolhead to the right end of the linear rail.
- Move the bed carriage block to the postion close to the third screw as shown in the picture.
  - ![Preview](img/10/0009-10.preview.png)
  [Large](img/10/0009-10.png)

### Position Bed V Holder

- Position the bed V holder as shown in the picture.
  - ![Preview](img/10/0010-8.preview.png)
  [Large](img/10/0010-8.png)

### Fold the Z Column

- Hold the bed V holder with one hand and fold the Z column with the other at the same time.
  - ![Preview](img/10/0011-5.preview.png)
  [Large](img/10/0011-5.png)

### Move Toolhead to the Z Column

- Move the toolhead back close to the Z column.
  - ![Preview](img/10/0012-2.preview.png)
  [Large](img/10/0012-2.png)

### Position the Screws

- Just put the thumb screws here and don&#x27;t need to screw them. You can unplug the toolhead cable as it might be in the way of the thumbscrew hole.
  - ![Preview](img/10/0015-6.preview.png)
  [Large](img/10/0015-6.png)
  - ![Preview](img/10/0013.preview.png)
  [Large](img/10/0013.png)

### A Tip

- Push ahead a little before you unfold the Z column.
  - ![Preview](img/10/0014-8.preview.png)
  [Large](img/10/0014-8.png)
